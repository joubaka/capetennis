Cape Tennis Code of Conduct

This Code applies to players, parents, guardians, coaches and spectators at Cape Tennis events, including conduct at the venue and in event-related messages and online discussions. Everyone must help create a safe, welcoming and fair tennis environment.

1. Respect and sportsmanship

  • Treat opponents, partners, officials, organisers, volunteers and spectators with courtesy. Recognise good play and compete with a positive attitude.
  • Do not use abusive language or gestures, threats, intimidation, bullying, harassment, discriminatory remarks or physical aggression.
  • Respect officials' instructions. Ask questions calmly and use the event's dispute procedure rather than arguing or confronting another player.

2. Fair play and honest scoring

  • Follow the rules and match format announced for the event. Do not cheat, deliberately misreport scores or manipulate results.
  • In matches without an umpire, make calls on your own side promptly and clearly. Give your opponent the benefit of the doubt: if you are unsure whether a ball is out, treat it as in.
  • Announce the score clearly before serving. Resolve scoring uncertainty calmly; ask an official for help if agreement cannot be reached.
  • Players should request an official's help with repeated line-call disputes. Parents, coaches and spectators must not make calls or intervene in scoring.

3. Safe conduct and care of facilities

  • Never throw a racket, strike a ball in anger or damage courts, equipment or property. Do not put anyone at risk.
  • Wear suitable tennis clothing and court-appropriate shoes. Keep courts clear of litter and return stray balls safely between points.
  • Wait for a suitable break before entering or crossing behind a court. Keep phones silent and avoid distracting players during points.
  • Follow venue and organiser instructions about weather, court safety and emergency procedures. Report injuries and unsafe conditions promptly.

4. Readiness and participation

  • Check the event's reporting time, schedule and updates. Be ready when called with the equipment and water needed to play.
  • Play at a reasonable pace and follow the applicable warm-up, rest and break rules. Ask an official before leaving the court during a match.
  • Notify the organiser promptly if you cannot attend, need to withdraw or cannot safely continue. Entry, withdrawal and refund conditions remain governed by the event's published terms.

5. Parents, guardians and spectators

  • Encourage effort, enjoyment, learning and fair play. Do not pressure, humiliate or punish a child for mistakes or a result.
  • Remain outside the playing area unless invited by an official. Applaud respectfully without celebrating an opponent's errors or disrupting play.
  • Do not coach during a match unless the event rules expressly permit it. Do not confront players, other families or officials.
  • Raise concerns privately with the organiser. Respect children's privacy and follow event requirements for photography and recording.

6. Coaches

Set a respectful example, support fair competition and put player wellbeing ahead of results. Maintain appropriate boundaries with children, respect officials and provide coaching only where the event rules allow it.

7. Reporting concerns

Report misconduct, bullying, abuse or safety concerns to the event organiser or referee as soon as possible. Give a factual account and any relevant evidence privately. Do not retaliate against someone who raises a concern in good faith or use social media to target a player, child or family. Seek immediate assistance where someone is in danger.

8. Consequences and review

Officials may address misconduct under the rules applicable to the event. Depending on the seriousness and circumstances, action may include a warning, an applicable match penalty, removal from the venue, match default or disqualification. Serious or repeated misconduct may be referred to Cape Tennis for review and possible suspension from future events. Serious safety incidents may require immediate action.

Concerns about a ruling should follow the event's referee or organiser process. Any further disciplinary review should give the person concerned an opportunity to respond and explain the decision and any available review process.

9. Acknowledgement

By participating in or attending a Cape Tennis event, I am expected to follow this Code and the event's published rules. Parents and guardians should explain these expectations to children in their care and model the same standards themselves.
