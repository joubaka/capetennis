# Player Plus automatic renewal

Player Plus offers annual or monthly automatic renewal through PayFast, or a once-off annual wallet purchase with no automatic renewal. The default monthly price is R69.00; the saved annual setting is untouched. Existing once-off annual orders keep their original terms and never become subscriptions. Only a super-user can save monthly pricing and offer availability. Monthly offers default off. Pausing general sales or monthly offers blocks the applicable new checkout/handoff but does not cancel agreed existing subscriptions or remove paid access.

## Reviewed scope

- Annual wallet purchases are once-off 12-month terms at the configured annual price, without automatic renewal. `PlayerPlusService::purchaseWithWallet()` atomically locks the player and payer wallet, checks unreleased registration/team/Plus reservations, records terms acceptance, and reserves/finalizes through `PaymentOrchestrator`. The ledger debit uses `player_plus_wallet_payment`; no PayFast handoff, receipt or paid flag is fabricated. `PlayerPlusOrder::verifiedSettlement()` requires exact, uniquely bound payer-wallet debit evidence before wallet orders grant access, appear on payer statements or generate managed confirmations. Existing reservation APIs outside Plus can still reserve raw balances; this feature does not claim to redesign those APIs. See `PlayerPlusWalletTest`.
- My Profile and the player dashboard link payers to My Plus orders: choose the subscription order, then Stop automatic renewal. Only the original payer can cancel; provider confirmation remains required and paid access continues until expiry. Wallet purchases have no renewal to cancel.

- The purchase journey uses Discover Player Plus → Choose a player → Choose a plan → Review & pay. Benefits live on the Discover page; selection and review retain the selected player, configured price and renewal terms. Plan selection/review happens in the browser before the purchase POST, which performs the canonical signed PayFast handoff or atomic wallet settlement according to the explicit payment choice. Switching plans clears consent. Without JavaScript, the original review forms remain available with required consents. Paused sales and existing-member progress access retain their server-side gates. See `player-plus-stepper.js`, `PlayerPlusLaunchTest` and `tests/Browser/player-plus-stepper.cjs`.

- Exact migration: `database/migrations/2026_11_01_180000_add_player_plus_monthly_subscriptions.php`, explicitly listed in `deploy.config`. Allowlisting is not permission to run it in production.
- Plans `monthly` and `annual_recurring` and recurring consent are immutable on the initial order and audited with terms version. Legacy `annual` orders remain once-off; new public PayFast requests for that plan are rejected, while explicit wallet purchases use `annual` with separate once-off wallet consent. Subscription checkout posts PayFast `subscription_type=1`, monthly `frequency=3` or annual `frequency=6`, unlimited `cycles=0`, and the first recurring `billing_date` one month or year ahead, while collecting the first amount now. [PayFast integration documentation](https://developers.payfast.co.za/) and [subscription API](https://developers.payfast.co.za/api/).
- Initial handoff uses the existing five-minute verified-payment window. Verified late initial payments are retained for reconciliation without access. Their provider mandate is stored as cancellation pending and a post-commit job attempts to stop it, with bounded retries. The original payer also has a stop-renewal action; support must resolve outstanding provider cancellation promptly if the provider does not confirm it.
- Only the shared signature/merchant verifier calls the Plus receipt handler. `COMPLETE`, exact immutable amount, original payer/player/order custom fields and a uniquely bound encrypted provider token are required. Browser returns never activate a term. Token hashes support unique binding; raw tokens never enter rendered pages, audit metadata or logs.
- Each accepted recurring payment creates its own immutable order and term, finalized through `PaymentOrchestrator`, with no wallet reservation/debit. A charge mapping joins that order to its provider receipt. The canonical receipt retains the originally signed root `custom_int5`, rather than relabelling incoming provider data. Payer statements and reconciliation resolve this mapping explicitly.
- Duplicate provider payment IDs cannot extend access again. A distinct early extra charge is reconciled without extending access. Once an entire expected cycle is missed, a later charge is reconciled and the subscription enters cancellation pending for payer/support resolution; it cannot remain silently active forever with an irrecoverable billing schedule.
- Periods follow the first recurring `billing_date` submitted to PayFast, using no-overflow month/year arithmetic from that provider anchor. For example, a 31 January initial purchase submits 28 February as its first recurring date, then subsequent monthly dates are 28 March and 28 April. A leap-day annual purchase first bills on 28 February the following year and keeps that first recurring anchor. Do not invent a later restoration of the original purchase day that differs from the provider schedule. Renewal never applies the initial checkout expiry, and arrival time never shifts the schedule.
- A payer cancellation request commits its intent and audit before calling the provider. Simultaneous verified charges are reconciled without extending access. API errors or ambiguous timeouts retain cancellation-pending status and existing paid access. Only an explicit successful provider cancellation or authenticated fetch showing the exact token and `CANCELLED` state marks renewal stopped. Tokens and request signatures are not logged. Repeated completed cancellation makes no further API call.
- Stopping renewal does not refund, transfer, alter a paid term, grant player ownership, or create a player link. An uncancelled mandate prevents a new subscription or legacy annual handoff for the same player. Missing effective merchant credentials/passphrase also blocks recurring handoff before storing handoff evidence. Automatic stop jobs use a system audit actor rather than impersonating the payer.
- Payer billing history, their order detail and stop-renewal remain available with authentication even if an agreement or player profile needs updating. These routes retain payer isolation and web CSRF; stopping renewal is throttled. Player discovery, private benefits, purchase and payment handoff retain their original agreement/profile gates. A stale player profile must not trap a payer in automatic charges.

## Before enabling offers

Confirm the migration and application are aligned, PayFast recurring billing is enabled for the intended merchant account, and the configured account/passphrase matches the environment. Verify signed checkout fields, the initial and next monthly charge, failed payment, cancellation, timeout/retry, duplicate ITN and payer isolation in the provider sandbox. Mocked application tests do not prove live PayFast acceptance. No external charge, cancellation, message, setting change or deployment is authorised by this document.

Run `PlayerPlusMonthlyTest`, existing `PlayerPlusMembershipTest`/`PlayerPlusLaunchTest`, deployment configuration tests, the complete feature suite for payment changes, route registration, Blade compilation and responsive browser QA. Preserve any independently existing work and report failures separately.
