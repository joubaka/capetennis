# Player Plus publication notifications

Local implementation; delivery is disabled by default. No live activation or historical backfill is implied.

## Morning information digest (local implementation, not activated)

The Plus marketing offer includes a daily check from **06:00 Africa/Johannesburg** for newly added public events, changed or added event information, and announcements the recipient has not marked as read. Send an email only when there is qualifying new or unseen information. New-event messages include the event count, dates, registration closing date where recorded, information excerpts and ordinary website links, helping busy parents follow registration and nomination dates without checking the site every day.

`PlusMorningDigestService`, `SendPlusMorningDigest` and `player-plus:morning-digest` implement this separately from the publication-only mechanism below. **Delivery defaults off** through `PLUS_MORNING_DIGEST_ENABLED`; no environment configuration or live mail is changed. Marketing describes the forthcoming benefit and excludes it from the current purchase until activation. There is no promise of exact inbox arrival or meeting a deadline.

- The scheduler uses the documented host ticks, every five minutes at :02/:07/:12 etc. Bounded capture starts at **03:12**, separate from the 03:02 ratings start, with continuations through 08:57. Accounts are prepared only after a scan completes on/after 06:00, normally starting at **06:02**; queue load and a large scan may delay delivery. This is code/schedule evidence, not confirmation of current production cron execution.
- Each invocation processes at most 100 public events, 100 public announcements and 50 eligible accounts by default. Persisted source cursors resume incomplete scans. Recipients are signed-in-account identities linked to active Plus players through `PlayerPlusEntitlement`, including current manual grants, excluding suspended/expired access and sponsor-only payers. Additional notification emails do not establish account identity or access. Account processing rotates by least recently processed so low IDs do not permanently starve later accounts.
- The first complete installation scan establishes a silent baseline. Each account's first eligible processing also establishes a baseline at the current change cursor; historical changes and changes captured before that enrollment baseline are deliberately not mailed. No mail is sent on an empty day. At most one digest is prepared per account per South African calendar day. Up to 25 current items from a bounded 100-change page are included; remaining changes retain their cursor position for later days.
- Public snapshots track event name, dates, registration close, general information, venue notes, signup state, organizer/public email, entry fee and withdrawal deadline. They also track public file-reference metadata, category fees/nomination-publication flags and venue-assignment metadata. Only the first 100 metadata rows per relation are projected, with total count and file maximum modification timestamp. Linked category or venue display-label changes, arbitrary settings, attachment byte changes without metadata updates, and edits beyond these bounds are not comprehensively tracked. No private nomination, player, roster, checkout or document contents enter the digest. Announcement revisions use `Announcement::readRevision()` and text-only excerpts.
- Opening a public event page while authenticated marks its event-information revision seen, **not** its collapsed announcements. Explicit announcement read/mark-all actions persist exact current revisions through an authenticated CSRF-protected endpoint. Stale/private/deleted revisions are rejected. Seen state belongs to the actor and hydrates across devices; sending a digest does not falsely mark a web announcement read.
- Before transport, the worker prunes items already read, hidden, removed or superseded, then rechecks current recipient email and Plus entitlement. A strict `MessageSending` listener revalidates the exact remaining public content, recipient, sender, reply address and absence of attachments/copies. Managed AMS mail and `outbound-mail` throttling are reused.
- A database-queue job and delivery reservation are created atomically on the application database. Unclaimed pending jobs can be requeued after 15 minutes, with locked single claim preventing duplicate transport. Old-date pending jobs are superseded; the unconsumed range remains available to the next day's digest. Definite pre-transport holds retain the range for a later day. Sent or transport-uncertain ranges are consumed atomically; uncertain acceptance and abandoned claimed deliveries never automatically resend. Operator reconciliation is required for those states. `sent` means transport acceptance, not proven inbox delivery.
- Migration `database/migrations/2026_11_20_120000_create_plus_morning_digests.php` is explicitly allowlisted in `deploy.config`; this does not authorize production migration or activation. Missing digest tables safely disable tracking. Focused coverage: `PlusMorningDigestTest`, `PlusAnnouncementReadTest`, `PlayerPlusScheduleTest`, and the announcement JavaScript tests.

## Account controls for morning emails

`/player-plus/notifications` is an authenticated own-account settings page, linked from My Tennis, Plus benefits and every morning digest. A CSRF-protected PATCH chooses daily conditional updates, a 7- or 30-day snooze, or off; submitted user identifiers never choose the owner. Expired members can still turn email off without purchasing again or passing unrelated profile gates. These choices affect only the morning digest, not payment receipts, registration confirmations or operational event communications.

`PlusDigestPreferences` is enforced during preparation, per-item validation, worker eligibility and the final managed-mail guard. Turning off or snoozing suppresses pending deliveries immediately; a preference change before the final transport check also prevents that message. Missing preference tables fail closed for morning email. Delivery already accepted by the mail transport cannot be recalled.

Snooze expiry and manual resume establish a **silent fresh baseline at the first eligible morning processing**, including changes first captured after the pause. Later processing can email newly captured changes. A scan timestamp does not prove when a source was edited, so a time cutoff alone cannot prevent a paused-period backlog. The durable `needs_baseline` flag and account cursor preserve this distinction; repeated saves of an already active daily preference do not reset the baseline. Existing silent enrollment, one-digest-per-account/day, no-empty-message and uncertain-transport safeguards continue to apply.

Migration `database/migrations/2026_11_20_180000_create_plus_digest_preferences.php` is separately allowlisted. Local implementation and tests do not activate mail or authorize migration/deployment. Focused regression coverage is in `PlusMorningDigestTest` (actor isolation, full authenticated settings rendering, off-after-queue, snooze expiry with unscanned changes, manual resume, repeated daily saves, and missing migration).

## Behaviour

An explicit draw or schedule publication records a durable per-draw public-content generation. Standard draws, Flexible Monrad, bulk publication and scoped schedule publication use the same notification service. The latest meaningful public change starts a **five-minute** quiet period. A further meaningful change restarts all five minutes; identical republishing and working schedule edits do not restart it. A minute scheduler prepares due messages, so transport is no earlier than five minutes and can be later under queue load.

Draw and schedule changes within the wait combine into one notice per affected player/contact. A shared family address can receive one notice for each affected player; addresses are normalized and deduplicated within each player/generation. Initial draw notices include all current active participants, including doubles partners. Schedule-only notices include participants of the changed, still-public fixtures. Team notices cover the current relevant team rosters (including competition substitutions and imported identities linked to a player profile), not a promise of individual rubber assignment. Cross-event sources and withdrawn individual entries cannot qualify.

If a new revision arrives while older notices are waiting for a worker, pending recipient scopes carry into the replacement generation. An unaffected player must not lose their unsent initial draw notice. Already-sent or transport-uncertain messages do not carry; those contacts receive only newly applicable changes. Every carried notice still requires current participation, Plus eligibility and a current contact relationship.

Public schedule fingerprints exclude publication timestamps, actor and draft values. `SchedulePublicationService` replaces timestamps even on identical republication, so timestamps and general schedule revisions are unsuitable deduplication keys. Event/draw hiding invalidates pending generations; hiding schedule slots removes those slots from the audience, and hiding every slot cancels a schedule-only notice. An otherwise pending public draw notice remains eligible. Previously tracked content becoming public again starts a fresh wait. Private previews do not start a timer. Event publication changes are observed only for already tracked draws, so ordinary old publications are never scanned into campaigns.

Changes to first-match/full-schedule visibility are treated as changes to the public projection. At due processing and before a worker claims delivery, a changed current public snapshot is reconciled into a fresh generation/wait rather than sending stale content. This also safely handles bracket participant progression during the wait or queue backlog. A stale queued delivery is suppressed; no stale generation is revived.

## Audience and links

Plus belongs to the player. Eligibility uses `PlayerPlusEntitlement::activePlayers()` at preparation and immediately before transport: verified current paid membership or current manual grant, excluding suspension and expiry. An unrelated Plus payer/sponsor is not implicitly a recipient. Contacts come from `TeamSelectionContactService::emails()`, including the player's valid primary/additional contacts and current linked/legacy account emails. Removed contacts, unlinked accounts without another current notification-contact basis, withdrawn players and revoked Plus access are suppressed at send time.

The primary button uses the ordinary authenticated `/my-tennis?player=<id>` route. Normal and two-factor login preserve this destination. Existing authorization still determines access to that player. Additional notification contacts may have no linked account and can use the public draw/schedule link instead. Email contains no invitation, checkout, clothing, automatic-login or access-granting token. Existing account links are not represented as verified guardianship.

## Operations and safeguards

- `config/publication_notifications.php` defaults `PLUS_PUBLICATION_MAIL_ENABLED` to false. No environment file is changed by this implementation. Explicit delivery activation is a separate authorized operation; it can process due changes already tracked since installation.
- `player-plus:publication-notifications --limit=50` prepares bounded due draws; it is scheduled every minute. It does not invoke transport directly. Delivery remains disabled until explicitly enabled.
- The database queue must share the application database. Delivery reservations and queued jobs are inserted atomically; rollback or queue submission failure leaves the campaign available for a later retry.
- `SendPlusPublicationNotification` uses managed mail and `outbound-mail` throttling. Recipient, current generation, content, sender and reply address are checked by the existing mail-review listener before transport. This narrow system-mail allowance does not authorize arbitrary event mail.
- A delivery is claimed once. Ambiguous transport exceptions become `acceptance_unknown`; a repeated worker cannot resend them automatically. Operator reconciliation is required before any manual resend. `sent` records transport acceptance, not inbox delivery.
- Migration: `database/migrations/2026_11_03_150000_create_plus_publication_notifications.php`, explicitly listed in `deploy.config`. The allowlist does not authorize production execution.
